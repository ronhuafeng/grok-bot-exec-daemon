does not authorize redistribution.

  distribution permission.

  time. Its license is the Node.js license distributed with that upstream
  archive. A checksum match is not a license grant.
- Third-party notices that arrived inside the imported snapshot stay next to

- Captured `rg`, `cursorsandbox`, Origin, tmux-root, `cursor-agent-store-fuse`,

  redistribution notice. Their status stays unresolved until a review supplies
